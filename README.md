# CPI-Inflation-Advance-excel

## Project Overview
This project analyzes **India’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) Inflation data from 2013 to 2023** using Microsoft Excel.  
The goal is to identify long-term inflation trends, category-level price movements, and derive **actionable economic and policy insights**.

The dataset covers **Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors** across **30+ CPI categories**.

## 📊 Dataset Details
- Time Period: 2013 – 2023  
- Rows: 372  
- Columns: 30  
- Sectors: Rural, Urban, Rural+Urban  
- Categories include:
  - Food & Beverages
  - Intoxicants
  - Fashion
  - Housing
  - Fuel and light
  - Household Goods and Services
  - Health
  - Transport and Communication
  - Recreation and Amusement
  - Education
  - Personal Care
  - Miscellaneous


## 🔎 Key Insights
- Food and beverages showed **high volatility**, especially vegetables and pulses  
- Rural inflation remained **consistently higher** than Urban inflation  
- Fuel and light prices had a strong cascading effect on transport and goods  
- Health and personal care costs increased steadily, especially after 2020  
- Pan, tobacco, and intoxicants experienced **sharp inflation growth**  
- Education and daily essentials became increasingly expensive  
- Major inflation spikes during 2020–2022 aligned with **global supply chain disruptions**



## 💡 Recommendations Based on Analysis
- Improve food storage, transport, and crop diversification  
- Promote renewable energy to reduce fuel dependency  
- Strengthen public healthcare to reduce personal expenses  
- Monitor taxation on intoxicants to control rapid price rise  
- Regulate education fees and essential goods pricing  
- Strengthen supply chains to handle global shocks
